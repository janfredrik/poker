# Surface: poker table (client/src/table)

Mode: Operate. Audience: friends at a remote home game, mostly on laptops, on video with each other. Task: act quickly on your turn, always know pot, stacks, whose turn, current blinds; host runs the clock, seats and ledger.
Constraints: up to 9 seats, English poker terms, standard web controls, phones must work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A friend's kitchen table at 22:00, not a casino. Faces sit around a fold-out felt and one lamp lights whoever has to act. Refuses the green casino oval with grey seat boxes.

OWN-WORLD: Walnut-brown ground (#3b2a20 family), teal felt topper as a rounded square, clay-chip denominations (bone, red, blue, green, black) as stack colours, lamp amber (#f2b350) as the only saturated control colour, reserved for what you can do now. Pencil-on-paper ledger drawer. Workhorse sans plus tabular numerals.

STORY: Players see who is at the table (faces), what is in the pot, what it costs them, and whose turn it is; they act in one click; the host sees clock and ledger without leaving the table.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top strip: table name, blind level bar (exact-length), next blinds. Centre: felt topper, five fixed board slots on one baseline, pot set huge as number plus chip pile. Around: nine seats as camera tiles with name, stack, bet chips in front. Bottom dock: hole cards, amount to call set large, Fold / Check-Call / Raise with slider in lamp amber. Side drawer: ledger, chat/log.

FORM: Kitchen Table Home Game, position 6 on the ordered list; seed key a13589ed. Signature move: the lamp, a warm pool of light that slides across the felt to the acting seat while the rest of the table sits in kitchen dimness; turn timer as an exact-length bar. Raises: action-only amber (warm consumer app), time as exact length (dance score), scale-only hierarchy (type specimen).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
